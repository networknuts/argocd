In configmap, argocd-rbac-cm, under namespace: argocd


data:
  policy.csv: |
    p, role:readonly-user, applications, get, default/myapp-*, allow
    p, role:readonly-user, applications, sync, default/myapp-*, allow
    p, role:readonly-user, applications, action/apps/Deployment/restart, default/myapp-*, allow
    p, role:readonly-user, exec, create, default/myapp-*, allow
    g, arnav, role:readonly-user
    g, aryan, role:readonly-user



So, our users “arnav & aryan” will have permission of view(get) the applications, sync the applications, restart the deployments, and exec i.e login to the terminal of the pod (if enabled exec) for all the applications whose name starts with “myapp-”
